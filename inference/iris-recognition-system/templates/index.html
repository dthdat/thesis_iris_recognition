ABOUT NOIR

Why the raw image is purple

A NoIR sensor has no infrared-cut filter. Near-infrared light reaches the red, green, and blue photosites in proportions the normal color pipeline was not designed to balance, so purple or magenta color is expected—not a network fault.

The NIR contrast preview converts the same raw frame to grayscale and applies local contrast for positioning. The original frame remains available and unchanged.
